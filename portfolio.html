TryNat – E-Commerce Platform

Natural and organic products marketplace.

Project overview

TryNat is an e-commerce platform for natural and organic products, delivered as a website and Android and iOS mobile applications with sales and digital marketing support.

Business problem

Customers looking for natural products need a convenient, trustworthy way to browse, order and receive them, while the business needs to manage catalogue, orders and delivery efficiently.

Technology stack

  • Java
  • Spring Boot
  • React.js
  • Next.js
  • RDBMS
  • Android
  • iOS

Key features

  • E-commerce website
  • Mobile applications
  • Payment integration
  • Order and delivery management

Website

www.trynat.com

Business outcome

A single platform that lets customers order across web and mobile while the team manages products, payments, orders and delivery in one place. Measured results will be published as they become available.

SkyFood – Food Delivery Platform

Food ordering and delivery management ecosystem.

Project overview

SkyFood is a food ordering and delivery ecosystem made up of Android and iOS applications for customers, restaurants and delivery partners.

Business problem

Ordering, preparing and delivering food involves several parties who need to stay in sync: customers want accurate tracking, restaurants need to manage incoming orders and delivery partners need clear tasks.

Technology stack

  • Android
  • iOS
  • Java
  • Spring Boot
  • RDBMS

Key features

  • Customer application
  • Restaurant management
  • Delivery partner application
  • Order tracking

Business outcome

One connected system for customers, restaurants and delivery partners, giving every party visibility of the order from placement to delivery. Measured results will be published as they become available.
